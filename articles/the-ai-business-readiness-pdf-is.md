# The AI Business Readiness PDF Is Ready

*17 AI Experts bridging the gap between business and tech - free to download*

Last week, I published the results of an expert validation survey: 17 AI practitioners ranked the questions every business leader should be asking their IT team about AI readiness.
The reactions said as much as the data. Two comments from the article stood out:

wrote: “The gap between IT and business on AI is where most companies quietly fail.”
 added, “While IT is buried in governance, non-technical leads are already out there building their own workarounds. If companies do not get ahead of that invisible build wave now, they are going to be drowning in technical debt in a year or two.”

That’s exactly why I built this PDF.
It’s a practical tool — the 16 questions ranked by expert consensus, an AI skill development journey, and the full list of contributors. You can print it, share it, or use it to open a conversation that might not be happening yet in your organization.
A few ways to use it:

Share it with your customers or prospects as a conversation starter

Bring it to your own leadership team or IT area

Upload it alongside the survey results to any LLM — ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and go deeper: find more research, build arguments, tailor the questions to your industry
