# Coexisting Intelligences — with Jenny Ouyang

*The unexpected side effects for humans that use AI*

Welcome to Automato’s special collaboration with Jenny Ouyang!
As many of you already know, Jenny is the creative force behind , a publication that helps people like you and me start building real solutions with AI. She’s not only a talented builder and creator, but also one of the kindest, most generous, and open-minded voices in the AI community here on Substack.

Jose: I’m definitely looking younger here :)
In this piece, we explore some of the deeper dimensions of the human–AI experience, how working with large language models is reshaping the way we think, create, and, perhaps most surprisingly, how these new human–AI interactions are redefining what it means to be human.
This collaboration was inspired by one of Jenny’s Notes that struck me deeply — a reflection on how AI reveals so much about ourselves. You can read it here:

Jose:
If you’ve read Jenny’s Note above, you might conclude that AI can teach us to be more kind.
That idea resonated deeply with me. I’ve long believed that we are raised to act out of fear, and that fear often distorts our perception of reality. In other words, we spend much of our lives trying to protect ourselves and our interests — while AI, in contrast, acts without fear, lack, or emotion.
Our interactions with AI mirror both our fears and our aspirations, revealing not only how we think, but also what we value most.
