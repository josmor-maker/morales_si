# Understanding Your Substack Audience Using AI

*Never Seen Before: A Workflow and Set of Prompts That Are Changing the Way I Work*

How do you know what your audience really wants? 
What is the urgent problem they want to solve? 
The question sounds simple, almost silly — but answering it can unlock value you didn’t even know you could provide.

Maybe it’s just me, but I find it extremely hard to match what I can offer with the formats people are ready to receive. I know the skills I have. I know the problems I can solve. But translating that into something that lands? That’s the tricky part.

Trying to identify a “painful and expensive problem” brought me to an unexpected realization:
If I want to find the problems that matter to my audience, I first need to understand their writing.

Because writing always reveals something deeper. 
Let me give you an example.
I discovered that I often write about community building because I believe community is the solution to many of my own challenges. And even though I have a community and years of experience building them, my writing shows something subtle: there is still a gap, something unresolved…
And that made me wonder:
If my writing reveals my hidden challenges, what is my audience’s writing revealing about theirs?
So the question became:
Can AI help me read between the lines?
After more than a week of experimentation, I can confidently say: yes, it can.
I used n8n, ChatGPT, and Google Sheets.
What I’m about to share is not a step-by-step tutorial on how to build the automation.
It’s the process that is helping me analyze what my potential customers actually need — in a way I had never seen before.
And here is the surprising part:
Even in the early data, I started seeing patterns I had never considered before.
