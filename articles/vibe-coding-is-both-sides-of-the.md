# Vibe Coding Is Both Sides of the Force

*Harness AI’s power while keeping your projects under control.*

As soon as a new technology or idea comes about, we see how new terminology and jargon appear. New job titles, new specializations, and honestly, a lot of b.s.
Everything becomes very overwhelming. But don’t be afraid! 
I’m about simplifying some of the new concepts so you feel at least well-informed, and hopefully ready to upskill.

This article is about Vibe Coding, and fortunately, we will clear up some other new names that are relevant and that you’ve probably heard many times.
What is vibe coding?
Vibe coding is the practice of building software by describing your intent in natural language and letting AI generate the code. Instead of writing every line, you “set the vibe”—the AI drafts, and you refine. It’s faster, more accessible, and creative, but it still requires oversight, testing, and human judgment.

A quick story
One designer recently tried Claude Code to build a small automation. At first, the app failed until he learned to treat the AI tool like a junior teammate. He gave context, reviewed the drafts, and nudged it back on track. The result wasn’t perfect, but it worked. The lesson? The vibe is only as good as the guidance you give.
