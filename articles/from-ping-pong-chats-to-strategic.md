# From Ping Pong Chats to Strategic Workflows: Custom GPTs and Actions

*How to Turn Everyday Routines Into AI-Supported Workflows — Before You Ever Automate Anything*

Tired of ping-pong chats with ChatGPT? Going back and forth, copying and pasting, losing track of what you’ve already refined?
If you’re spending hours in repetitive conversations that never quite become strategic, you’re not alone.

Yes, one step up is using automation tools like Zapier, Make, or n8n.
But there is a previous step — one available to all paying ChatGPT users, and often overlooked.
I’m talking about Custom GPTs and Actions.
What you’ll learn in this post: How to move beyond basic ChatGPT conversations into strategic automation using Custom GPTs and Actions. I’ll show you how I built a system that categorizes, rewrites, and saves my Substack Notes to a Notion database — cutting my workflow from 2 hours to 30 minutes. No coding required, just clear instructions and a bit of patience. If you’re a content creator, marketer, or anyone managing repetitive text-based work, this will open new possibilities for your own workflows.

There’s a great guide about Custom GPTs by MIT, and another by Zapier.
But let me give you the essence:

A Custom GPT is a version of ChatGPT you design with your own instructions.
Your instructions shape its behavior, its tone, its abilities — making it useful for specific tasks that matter to you.
