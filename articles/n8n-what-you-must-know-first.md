• Workflows are highways connecting districts
• Nodes are buildings where work happens
• Data is traffic flowing between buildings
• Triggers are traffic lights starting the flow
• Credentials are security passes for buildings
• Executions are completed journeys
This mental model transforms complexity into clarity!
