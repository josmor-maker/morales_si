# Your Domain Knowledge Is the Product

*MCP is how you ship it*

TLDR

The real opportunity in the AI era is not learning to use AI to go and build faster. It is encoding your unique domain knowledge — your methodology, your judgment, your years of experience — into a process that clients can run on their own. MCP (Model Context Protocol) makes this possible: it is the connector between the AI chat interface and a process layer that runs your unique perspective as a system. The business model that follows is simple. Clients access your thinking at scale. You show up only where no process can replace you.

There is a new kind of business becoming possible. Not a better version of what already exists — something structurally different. And I only saw it clearly after sitting in one of Jenny Ouyang’s sessions last week.
 showed us one of her MCP servers. It handles research. You give it a topic, and the whole process runs in the background — a set of agents, a set of skills, a methodology she built over time. For the user, it feels like chatting. But underneath, it is Jenny’s way of thinking doing the work.
That moment cracked something open for me.

The model most solopreneurs are building is already obsolete
The current conversation about AI for independent professionals is mostly about speed. Use AI to write faster. Use AI to research faster. Use AI to do in one hour what used to take five.
That is fine. But it is not the real opportunity.
Before I get to the opportunity, let me draw a simple picture.
The LLM chat interface — Claude, ChatGPT, whatever you use — is becoming the new operating system. Not for your computer. For your work. The way a browser became the interface for the internet, the chat window is becoming the interface for knowledge work. You type. Things happen. You do not see the infrastructure underneath.
MCP — Model Context Protocol — is the layer that connects that interface to systems, tools, and processes running behind it. It is the protocol that makes the chat window powerful. When Jenny’s research MCP is connected, asking Claude to run a landscape scan is not just a prompt anymore. It is an instruction that triggers a whole structure — agents, skills, sources — invisible to the user, visible only in the quality of what comes back.
