# I built an AI-assisted Knowledge Management system for you

*Claude, Obsidian, and keeping your knowledge local*

**TL;DR**

- I built my own AI-assisted knowledge management system — I call it AKM — using Obsidian, Claude, and a custom 3x3 grid. It's not a Second Brain; I'm still doing the thinking.
- This article walks through why I built it, how it's structured, what Claude actually does versus what I do myself, and what's still missing from it. You can build it yourself using this guide.
- Premium members get the full system: four copy-paste Claude prompts that build your own 3x3 grid, your own starting themes, your own vault structure, and a scheduled check that keeps it all honest over time — plus an installable Claude Skill that files your writing into it automatically from then on.
- None of it requires copying my exact grid, my themes, or my setup. Every prompt walks you through defining your own, based on what you actually write about.
- You'll need Claude Cowork (Pro plan or higher) for the vault-building, filing, and scheduled-check steps, since those write real files and folders on your computer.
