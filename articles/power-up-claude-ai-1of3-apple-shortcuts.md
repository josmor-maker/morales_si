# Power Up Claude AI (1/3): Apple Shortcuts

*The overlooked tool, before the rush begins.*

**TL;DR**

- **Who it’s for:** Mac users who already use Claude.
- **Part 1 of 3.** This article covers what workflow automation is, the three words that unlock Apple Shortcuts (*Shortcut*, *Action*, *Trigger*), and a prompt that turns Claude into your tutor to build your first automation: a text message whenever a file on your Mac changes. No code, no cost.
- **The whole series:** you’ll learn one way to integrate Claude and Shortcuts, and then how to connect a web service like Bluesky to Claude, using its API and a custom-built MCP server. It sounds more complicated than it is.  [Subscribe now](https://automato.substack.com/subscribe?)

Every morning I want the same thing from the news: a summary. I want to avoid ads, browsing, and the snapshots of terrible things the news media industry loves to share to keep our attention. So I thought: With AI, this will be so simple. Claude reads the news I like; Claude summarises it. Done.

**It wasn’t.**

Asking Claude to browse news sites every morning turned out to be slow, unreliable, and expensive in credits. And Claude couldn’t read RSS feeds on its own. RSS is the old, humble format news sites use to send out their headlines.
