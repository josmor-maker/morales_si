# Can Notion Really Run Your Business?

*Interview with Vito Meznaric, a Notion Expert*

Have you used Notion? If not, you’ve probably heard about it. But here’s the real question: can a tool that looks so simple actually power complex workflows—or even run a whole business?

To explore that, I turned to my friend Vito. He has a rare gift for turning complexity into efficiency, and this interview shows exactly how he does it.
Let me know if you decide to give Notion a try!

A Conversation with Vito Meznaric, a Notion expert user
Hi Vito, tell us a bit about you, your work, and your story with Notion.
Hey Jose, I’m a Notion power user and have been using it for years. I’ve built my own products on top of Notion and used it in a professional context in at least 10 projects. Sometimes we used it as a knowledge base, but most often it was a knowledge base plus a ticketing system

Vito Meznaric
.For every project I start, I usually begin with a simple Notion page and then build the systems out based on the project and team requirements.

Why is Notion an option for workflow automation?
Vito: I think the main reason is how flexible it is. Do you want a basic Kanban board? Or a really advanced one? Or something completely different, let’s say a client management system for filmmakers? It has you covered.
Beyond that, it allows you to do some basic automations inside Notion itself, and it also exposes an API so you can create much more powerful integrations. Although I would say the API does require a developer to work with (or maybe some people can vibe-code integrations with AI?).
