What is NotebookLM good for?
Think of it as a focused version of ChatGPT with built-in additional tools.
Instead of working in loose chats, NotebookLM works with Notebooks.
Each notebook contains sources, your documents, links, videos, or research notes. The AI reads those sources and uses them as context for your requests: summarizing, analyzing, comparing, or answering questions.
In other words, your notebooks become thematic containers powered by an AI that learns only from what you feed it.
Yes, ChatGPT lets you create Custom GPTs, but you have to train them before they perform well. NotebookLM skips that step; the “training” happens automatically as soon as you upload your materials.
