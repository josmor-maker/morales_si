# Fake Musk, an Austrian Chicken, and What Comes After Salaries

*What a debunked Musk quote and a century-old chicken breed taught me about self-reliance.*

A friend [Karo (Product with Attitude)](https://open.substack.com/users/27968736-karo-product-with-attitude) shared a quote in her Substack Chat attributed to an Elon Musk interview:

"At most, there are 3 years left in which you can still make money selling your work. After that, AI will automate most tasks, and paying a person for their time will no longer make sense. The model of the last 200 years—'I give you your salary in exchange for your time'—is coming to an end. This will trigger the greatest transfer of wealth in modern history..."

I couldn't find the source of that quote, so I have no clue if Musk is really behind it. Karo later confirmed that Musk isn't the author of the text, but the text seems very interesting for a visionary conversation.

Karo's question changed my day:

**"Are you already transforming your time into scarce assets, or are you still waiting for it to become more obvious?"**

That question made me remember all the conversations I had with my friends about the future of humanity after Artificial General Intelligence is achieved. I'm sure you've had similar deep dives, and *I'd love to know what you think about the future of money*.
