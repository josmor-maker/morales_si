# A Digitalization Case Study for Production Businesses

*From no IT area, through workflow automation, to AI implementation*

TL;DR Growing companies accumulate informal systems that work, until they don't. Spreadsheets nobody can explain, processes nobody has documented, technology decisions made in the wrong order. There is a specific sequence to fix this. This is what it looks like in practice.

Every company has a version of this story: The company starts with a few team members, grows fast, the business develops around the first customers, the complexity expands, and as time passes, some inefficiencies stay in place.
This story, similar to most businesses, starts with a phone call.

“Jose, we have a problem with our SharePoint.”

The problem was not SharePoint
A few years ago, someone I had worked with on a SharePoint project many years before called me. His company had been trying to solve the same technology problem for five years. 
Our first meeting made one thing clear: the team had been treating SharePoint as a filing cabinet. The complexity and the number of SharePoint sites were overwhelming, and the information stored there was not usable. 
When I started asking how information moved through the company, something else emerged.
There was no standard for how information was created, stored, or shared. The different business areas had their own rules; the documents lived in personal notebooks, local drives, cloud folders, and Excel files that only a few people knew how to use. When management requested a report, people knew roughly where to look. “Roughly” is not a system.
